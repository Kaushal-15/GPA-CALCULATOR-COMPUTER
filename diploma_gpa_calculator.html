Regulation 2023 Scheme Details

The Directorate of Technical Education (DOTE) Tamil Nadu governs the Curriculum and Grading system for diploma courses in computer engineering under Regulation 2023.

Grading & Grade Points System

  • O Grade (Outstanding): 10 Grade Points
  • A+ Grade (Excellent): 9 Grade Points
  • A Grade (Very Good): 8 Grade Points
  • B+ Grade (Good): 7 Grade Points
  • B Grade (Above Average): 6 Grade Points
  • C Grade (Satisfactory): 5 Grade Points
  • RA (Re-Appearance): 0 Grade Points

Entry Options

  • Regular Entry: Students complete 6 semesters starting from Semester I. Total credits are accumulated across all subjects.
  • Lateral Entry: Eligible students join directly in the second year (Semester III). Semesters I and II are excluded from both credit and CGPA evaluations.

Advanced Skills Certification (ASC)

Certain semesters include Advanced Skills Certifications (ASC) marked with (NA) option. These courses can be marked as "NA" (Not Applicable) if they were not taken, in which case their credits are bypassed.

Formula Guidelines

Semester GPA (SGPA)

SGPA = Σ (Course Credits × Grade Points) Σ (Course Credits)

Cumulative GPA (CGPA)

CGPA = Σ (All Earned Points) Σ (All Course Credits)

Division Standards

  • First Class with Distinction: CGPA ≥ 7.50
  • First Class: CGPA ≥ 6.50
  • Second Class: CGPA ≥ 5.00
